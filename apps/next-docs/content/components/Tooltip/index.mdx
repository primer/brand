## Usage

Use a tooltip to provide a short label or a small amount of supplementary context when someone focuses or points to an interactive control. Reserve tooltips for information that helps in the moment but is not essential to completing a task.

Tooltips are hidden until someone interacts with their trigger, and they may be difficult to discover on touch devices or while browsing with a screen reader. Keep essential instructions and information visible on the page.

Use tooltips primarily to clarify familiar icon-only actions. Avoid adding a tooltip when a control already has a clear visible label.

## Content

Keep tooltip text concise and specific. When the tooltip labels an action, begin with a clear verb. When it adds context to an existing label, include only supplementary, non-essential information.

Do not use a tooltip for long instructions, structured content, or information that someone must read before continuing.

## Interaction

A tooltip appears when its trigger receives keyboard focus or when a pointer rests over it. It remains visible while the trigger or tooltip is being pointed at, and it closes when focus or the pointer moves away. Pressing Escape also closes it without moving focus.

Use a longer delay for dense groups of familiar controls when immediate tooltips would be distracting. Keyboard focus should always reveal the tooltip immediately.

## Accessibility

- Attach a tooltip only to a control that performs an action or navigation.
- Do not attach a tooltip to static text or imagery.
- Do not attach a tooltip to a disabled control because it cannot receive keyboard focus. If an explanation is necessary, keep the control focusable while preventing the unavailable action.
- Keep labels concise and descriptive of the control's purpose.
- Keep supplementary descriptions non-essential because not everyone will discover them.
- Ensure the trigger has a visible focus indicator.

## Placement

Place a tooltip next to its trigger so their relationship is visually clear. Tooltips appear below their trigger by default but may move to another side when space is limited. Do not rely on a specific placement to communicate meaning.

## Alternatives

Before adding a tooltip, consider whether the information can remain visible on the page. Use a dialog for longer or structured content, or a disclosure when people need to reveal and hide supporting information.
