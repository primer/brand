Added the new `MinimalVideoPlayer` component for presenting muted, looping videos with an accessible play and pause control. It automatically pauses when outside the viewport and respects reduced motion preferences.

```tsx
<MinimalVideoPlayer poster="/video-poster.jpg" src="/video.webm" title="Product interface demonstration" />
```

🔗 [See `MinimalVideoPlayer` documentation for more usage examples](https://primer.style/brand/components/MinimalVideoPlayer)
